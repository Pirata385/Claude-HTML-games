Field guide

An endless world teems with procedurally evolved insects, mammals, reptiles, birds and chimerids. Find them, scan them, name them and fill your bestiary.

Touch

Drag anywhere to walk (push far to run).

Tap the ground to walk there · tap a creature to target it.

Hold SCAN near a target to analyse it.

CALL whistles: curious creatures approach, shy ones flee.

Pinch to zoom · arrow buttons rotate the view.

Keyboard

WASD move · Shift run

Space hold to scan · Tab next target

F call · Q/E rotate · +/− zoom

B bestiary · Esc menu · H help

Tips

A blinking ? marks an unknown species; edge markers point to unknown creatures off-screen. Each region and biome has its own populations, so keep travelling to find new species. Some creatures only come out at night, when your lantern lights the way. Progress saves automatically.
